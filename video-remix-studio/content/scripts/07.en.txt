Most variance analysis says what changed. Very little of it says why.
Revenue is two hundred thousand below budget. That's not an explanation. That's the question.
Split it into three drivers. Price: did we sell for less? Volume: did we sell fewer units? Mix: did we sell more of the cheaper products? Now the gap has causes, and every cause has an owner.
Then filter. Not every variance deserves a paragraph. Set a threshold, for example five percent and a minimum amount, and explain only what crosses it.
Show it as a waterfall chart, from budget to actual, one bar per driver. Your CFO reads it in ten seconds.
AI can draft the commentary from the bridge table. Your job is to add what the numbers can't know, like the customer who delayed an order.
