You don't need a hundred AI tips. You need five habits you actually use.
Habit one: always give context. Who you are, who the output is for, and what good looks like.
Habit two: give it your data, not your memory. Paste the table, upload the file.
Habit three: ask for the format you need. A table, three bullets, an email under a hundred words.
Habit four: make it check itself. Ask what assumptions it made and what could be wrong.
Habit five: save what works. A good prompt you reuse every month is worth more than ten clever ones you forget.
And one rule above all: never send a number you didn't verify yourself.
Five habits, every day. That's how finance teams really get faster with AI.
