Four ways finance teams should use Claude.
Long documents: covenants and obligations in a table, with page references.
Spreadsheets: explain a model, find broken formulas.
Small tools: a calculator or a dashboard you can share.
Projects: your policies and templates in one place.
Use the approved version, and verify every number.
