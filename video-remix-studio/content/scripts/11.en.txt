Four ways finance teams should be using Claude.
One: long documents. Give it the full annual report, a loan agreement, or a set of contracts, and ask for covenants, obligations and risks in a table, with page references so you can check.
Two: spreadsheets. Upload the file and ask it to explain a complex model, find broken formulas, or build a summary sheet.
Three: artifacts. Ask for a small dashboard or calculator, and it builds one you can use and share.
Four: projects. Keep your policies, templates and past work in one place, so every answer follows your house style.
The same rule applies as with any AI tool: work in the approved business version, and verify every number before it leaves your desk.
