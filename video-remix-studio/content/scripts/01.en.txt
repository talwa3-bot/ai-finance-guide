Most finance teams don't fail at AI because of the tools. They fail because nobody gave them a plan.
Here's a 30-day plan you can start on Monday.
Week one: pick one secure tool the company approves, and give everyone the same three starter prompts. Summarize, explain, draft.
Week two: each person chooses one task they hate. A reconciliation note, a variance comment, a supplier email. They use AI on it every day.
Week three: share what worked. Fifteen minutes in the team meeting. Save the best prompts in one shared document.
Week four: measure. Hours saved, errors caught, and what still needs a human check.
One rule throughout: AI drafts, people sign.
After thirty days you don't have an AI project. You have a team that uses AI every day.
