Your finance team doesn't need more AI tools. It needs a 30-day plan.
Week one: one approved tool, three shared prompts.
Week two: everyone automates one task they hate.
Week three: share what worked.
Week four: measure the hours saved.
One rule: AI drafts, people sign.
Follow AI Finance for the full plan.
