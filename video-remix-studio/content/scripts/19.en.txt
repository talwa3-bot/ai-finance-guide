Not every variance deserves your time. Here's how to decide which ones to ignore.
Draw a simple matrix. On one axis: how big is the variance? On the other: can anyone actually control it?
Big and controllable: explain it, assign an owner, agree on an action. This is where your time goes.
Big but not controllable, like exchange rates or a regulatory change: report it clearly, once, and move on.
Small but controllable: watch the trend. If it repeats three months in a row, it moves up.
Small and not controllable: ignore it. Really.
Set the thresholds in advance with your CFO, so nobody argues about it at month-end.
AI can sort every variance into the matrix in seconds. You decide what to do with the top-right corner.
