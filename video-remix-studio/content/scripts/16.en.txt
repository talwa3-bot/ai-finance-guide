Want to start learning AI as a finance professional? Don't start with a course. Start with a task.
Step one: pick one real task from your week. A variance comment, a reconciliation note, a summary for your manager.
Step two: do it with AI every time it comes up, for one month. Write down what worked and what didn't.
Step three: learn the basics around it. How to give context, how to check output, what data you may and may not share.
After one month you'll know more than after ten videos, because you learned on your own numbers.
Then pick the next task. That's the whole method.
And if you want to learn with other finance people who are doing the same, join the AI Finance community.
