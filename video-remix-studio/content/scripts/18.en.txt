Your CFO doesn't want thirty slides every month. They want five.
Slide one: the headline. Three sentences. What happened, why, and what we're doing about it.
Slide two: results versus budget. Revenue, gross margin, operating profit, with the main reasons for each gap.
Slide three: cash. Where it is today, where it will be in three months, and any risk to it.
Slide four: the forecast. Has the full-year number changed, and why?
Slide five: decisions needed. What do you need from management this month?
Everything else goes to the appendix.
One rule for every slide: the title is the conclusion, not the topic. Not revenue analysis, but revenue missed budget because two deals slipped to next quarter.
AI can turn your analysis into a first draft of these five slides. You make sure each title is true.
