# AI skills: turn your best process into a reusable tool [en] Instagram Reel, look 23
# Original short script inspired by the topic of: Claude Skills for Finance Are Here and Most People Are Using Them Wrong (Moonshine Capital TV) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hXYtY6BYgQk
# Lines starting with # are notes and are not spoken.
Stop retyping the same AI instructions every month.
Write your best process down once: the steps, the format, the tone, and one great example.
Save it as a skill or as project instructions.
Now each month you only add the new numbers.
Same quality, every time, whoever runs it.
The value isn't the AI. It's the process you wrote.
