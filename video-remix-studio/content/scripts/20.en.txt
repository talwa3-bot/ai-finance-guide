# AI skills: turn your best process into a reusable tool [en] presenter=tal
# Original script inspired by the topic of: Claude Skills for Finance Are Here and Most People Are Using Them Wrong (Moonshine Capital TV) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hXYtY6BYgQk
# Lines starting with # are notes and are not spoken.
Most people use AI the same way every time. They retype the same instructions, month after month.
There's a better way: turn your best process into a reusable skill.
Take one process you do every month. For example, preparing the variance commentary.
Write it down once. The steps, the format, your thresholds, the tone your CFO likes, and an example of a great result.
Save it as a skill, or as instructions in a project, depending on the tool you use.
Now every month you just give it the new numbers. Same quality, same format, in minutes.
Even better: the whole team uses the same skill, so the output is consistent no matter who prepares it.
The value isn't in the AI. It's in the process you wrote down.
