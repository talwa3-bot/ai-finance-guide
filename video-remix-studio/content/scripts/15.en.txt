Where does vibe coding actually help finance?
Small internal tools: matching two files, commission calculators, a monthly dashboard.
Where doesn't it? Anything that becomes a system of record: the ledger, payroll, tax.
The test: if the tool breaks, can you still close the month?
If yes, build it. If not, talk to IT first.
