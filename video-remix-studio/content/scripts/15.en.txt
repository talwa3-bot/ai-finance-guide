Vibe coding is everywhere. Here's where it actually helps finance, and where it doesn't.
Where it helps: small internal tools. A reconciliation helper that matches two files. A calculator for commission or lease schedules. A dashboard that reads your monthly export. A tool that turns a trial balance into a first draft of the notes.
Where it doesn't: anything that becomes a system of record. The general ledger, payroll, tax filings. Those need controls, audit trails and owners.
The test is simple. If the tool breaks, can you still close the month? If yes, build it. If no, talk to IT first.
And always: test with data you know, keep sensitive data out until the tool is approved, and write down how it works so it doesn't depend on one person.
