Five dashboards every CFO should have.
One: cash. Opening balance, inflows, outflows, and a thirteen-week forecast. Cash is the first question in every crisis.
Two: profit and loss versus budget. Monthly and year to date, with the three biggest variances explained.
Three: receivables. Aging, days sales outstanding, and the top ten overdue customers.
Four: unit economics. Gross margin by product or customer, so you see where you really make money.
Five: headcount and payroll. Actual versus plan, open positions, cost per department.
Keep each dashboard to one screen. If it needs scrolling, it needs editing.
AI can help you build the first version and write the monthly commentary, but the definitions must be agreed once and never change quietly.
