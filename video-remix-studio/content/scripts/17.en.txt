# 5 dashboards every CFO should have [en] Instagram Reel, look 23
# Original short script inspired by the topic of: 5 Excel Dashboards EVERY CFO Needs (Josh Aharonoff (Your CFO Guy)) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9GFSw7xcsvw
# Lines starting with # are notes and are not spoken.
Five dashboards every CFO should have.
Cash, with a thirteen-week forecast.
Profit and loss versus budget.
Receivables and the top overdue customers.
Margin by product or customer.
Headcount and payroll versus plan.
One screen each. If it needs scrolling, it needs editing.
