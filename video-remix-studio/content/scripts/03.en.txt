Every strong financial analysis starts with three simple methods.
Method one: horizontal analysis. Compare the same line over time. Revenue up twelve percent is a fact. Revenue up twelve percent while receivables grew thirty is a story.
Method two: vertical analysis. Show every line as a percentage of revenue. Suddenly you see that marketing went from eight to eleven percent of sales.
Method three: ratios. Margins, liquidity, days sales outstanding, return on capital. They connect the income statement to the balance sheet.
Here's where AI helps. Give it the statements and ask for all three methods as a first pass, with the five lines that moved the most. You still check every number, but you start from insight, not from a blank sheet.
The numbers tell you what happened. These three methods tell you why.
