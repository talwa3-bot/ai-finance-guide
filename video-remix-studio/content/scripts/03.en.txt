Three methods behind every good financial analysis.
Horizontal: the same line over time.
Vertical: every line as a percentage of revenue.
Ratios: margins, liquidity, days to collect.
Ask AI for all three as a first pass, plus the five lines that moved the most.
You still verify every number, but you start from insight.
