There isn't one financial model. There are seven, and each answers a different question.
One: the three-statement model. Income statement, balance sheet and cash flow, linked. The base for almost everything.
Two: the budget model. What do we plan to spend and earn next year?
Three: the forecast model. Given what we know today, where will we land?
Four: the discounted cash flow model. What is the business worth?
Five: the M and A model. Does this acquisition create value for us?
Six: the leveraged buyout model. How much debt can this deal carry?
Seven: the project evaluation model. Should we invest in this project at all?
Before you build anything, write down the decision the model supports. That decides the model, the level of detail, and the time it deserves.
