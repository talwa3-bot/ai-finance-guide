Most finance people use ChatGPT like a search engine. Here are four moves that change that.
Move one: projects. Keep a project for month-end with your chart of accounts, your policies and last month's notes. Every chat starts with context.
Move two: let it analyze the file, not the screenshot. Upload the export and ask for a pivot, a trend, or the ten largest movements.
Move three: build prompts you reuse. Your variance comment prompt, your supplier email prompt, your board summary prompt. Same structure, every month.
Move four: ask for a review, not just an answer. Here's my analysis, what did I miss, and what would an auditor question?
And keep the boundary clear. Use the business version your company approves, and keep sensitive data out of personal accounts.
