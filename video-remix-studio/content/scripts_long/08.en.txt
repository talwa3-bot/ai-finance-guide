You can build a finance app today without writing a single line of code.
It's called vibe coding. You describe the tool you want in plain language, and an AI builds it.
Example: a variance dashboard. Upload a budget file and an actuals file, show the gaps by department, flag anything above five percent, and draft a comment for each.
Three rules before you start.
One: use sample or anonymized data until the tool is approved. Never upload client or payroll data to a tool your company hasn't cleared.
Two: test it against numbers you already know. If the app says marketing is over budget, check it in the source file.
Three: keep it small. Internal helpers, not the general ledger.
Vibe coding won't replace your systems. It replaces the spreadsheet you rebuild every month.
