Five finance tasks Copilot can do in Excel today.
One: clean data. Split columns, fix dates, remove duplicates from an ERP export, just by asking.
Two: write formulas. Describe what you need, for example sum of sales by customer for the last quarter, and get the formula with an explanation.
Three: build pivots and charts. Ask for revenue by region and month, and get the table and the chart.
Four: find outliers. Ask which expenses are unusual compared to the last six months.
Five: draft commentary. Ask for three key insights from the table to start your management note.
Two tips. Format your data as a table first, because Copilot works best on structured data. And check every formula it writes before you build on it.
