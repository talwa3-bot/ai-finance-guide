If your company runs on Microsoft, Copilot is already sitting inside the tools you use every day. Here are five finance moves.
One: in Excel, ask it to explain a formula, highlight outliers, or build a pivot from a messy table.
Two: in Outlook, summarize a long email thread with a supplier and draft the reply.
Three: in Teams, get the decisions and action items from the budget meeting you missed.
Four: in Word, turn your analysis into a management memo with a clear structure.
Five: in PowerPoint, build the first draft of the monthly review from your summary.
Copilot works inside your company's security boundary, which makes it the easiest place to start.
But the review step doesn't change. You check every number and every sentence before it goes out.
Which one would you start with? Tell me in the comments.
