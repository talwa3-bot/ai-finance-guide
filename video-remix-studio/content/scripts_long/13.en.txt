AI can build a financial model in minutes. That's exactly why you need rules.
Rule one: you write the assumptions. Growth, prices, payment terms. AI builds the structure around them.
Rule two: ask for a clean layout. Inputs on one sheet, calculations on another, outputs on a third.
Rule three: no hard-coded numbers inside formulas. Ask for it explicitly, then check.
Rule four: ask for built-in checks. The balance sheet balances, cash ties to the cash flow, totals match.
Rule five: test it. Change one assumption and see if the results move the way you expect.
Used this way, AI saves you hours of setup, and you spend your time where it matters: on the assumptions and the story behind them.
A model you can't explain is a model you can't defend.
